## 🏗️ Projects

### 🧮 [NomadTaxCalc – Multi-Country Tax Calculator](https://nomadtaxcalc.com/) *(Laravel · React · Inertia.js · MySQL)*
📅 **Nov 2025 – Present**
A free tax liability calculator for digital nomads and expats — built end-to-end as a personal SaaS product.
- 3-step wizard: income & citizenship → residency details (183-day rule) → tabbed results
- Progressive tax bracket engine with FEIE, Foreign Tax Credit & treaty detection
- Scenario Comparison Tool: side-by-side "what-if" analysis with winner banners & delta KPIs
- Verified 2025 & 2026 bracket data for 150+ countries (Spain, UAE, UK, Georgia, USA, Panama…)
- Monetised via Google AdSense; SEO blog on [https://nomadtaxcalc.com/blog/](https://nomadtaxcalc.com/blog/)

**Blog theme repo:** [`nomadtaxcalc-blog-wordpress`](https://github.com/AhmarIftikhar123/nomadtaxcalc-blog-wordpress)
> Blocksy child theme with Vite + Dart Sass (7-1 pattern), PSR-4 Composer autoloading, custom Reading Progress Bar, configurable TOC shortcode, and integrated Mega Menu.
